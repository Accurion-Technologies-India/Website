About Us

Who We Are

Delhi's Trusted Civil Lab Equipment Partner

Accurion Technologies is a trusted manufacturer, supplier, trader, and service provider of complete Civil Quality Laboratory Equipment and NABL-accredited calibration solutions. Based in Delhi, we proudly serve customers across India with high-quality testing instruments, laboratory setup solutions, calibration support, and technical assistance.

We specialise in the setup of complete civil engineering laboratories for testing of Concrete, Cement, Soil, Bitumen, Aggregates, Steel, Water, and Construction Materials. From supplying testing equipment to installation guidance and after-sales support, we provide end-to-end laboratory solutions as per project and quality requirements.
