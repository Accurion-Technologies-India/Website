⏱️ 6 Steps to Publish an Instrument

1

Open Equipment Catalogue

Log in to /admin/ with GitHub. Click Equipment Catalogue in the left sidebar, then click the blue New Equipment button.

2

Fill in Commercial Details

Type the official commercial product name, model code, and select the category:

  • Product Name: e.g. Langry RH225-B Integrated Digital Rebound Hammer
  • Product Code / SKU: e.g. RH225-B
  • Category: Choose from dropdown (e.g. NDT Equipment)
  • Short Description: 1–2 sentences for search result snippets and catalogue cards.
3

Upload Featured Image (Zero-Crop Guarantee)

Click Choose an image to upload from your computer or phone.

🛡️ 4-Layer Image Defense: You do not need to edit or crop photos! Our system automatically centers the instrument on a studio-neutral canvas, auto-rotates phone pictures, and compresses files below 150 KB for blazing fast loading.
4

Add Technical Specifications

Under Technical Specifications, click + Add specification to add key parameters:

  • Impact Energy: 2.207 J
  • Measuring Range: 10 to 60 MPa
  • Applicable Standards: IS 13311 (Part 2), ASTM C805, EN 12504-2

Click + Add specification as many times as needed to add more rows.

5

Add Key Features & Brochure PDF

Under Key Features, add bullet points highlighting unique advantages (e.g. Integrated OLED screen, Bluetooth wireless printer link). Optionally upload your official PDF product brochure.

6

Review Split-Screen Preview & Publish

Inspect the live card preview on the right side of your screen. When ready, ensure status is set to Published and click the green Publish button!

The automated static site generator compiles the product page, updates the search index, and updates sitemaps automatically.

💬 Managing Customer Quotations & Leads

CRM

One-Click WhatsApp & Lead Tracking at /admin/enquiries/

All quote requests submitted through the website appear instantly in the CRM inbox:

  • Instant WhatsApp: Click the green 💬 WA button next to any enquiry to open WhatsApp with a pre-filled, courteous quotation message.
  • Status Pipeline: Move leads through New → Contacted → Quoted → Closed.
  • Export to Excel: Click 📥 Export CSV at any time to download all customer inquiries for your sales records.

❓ Common Questions

Can I save an instrument as a draft?

Yes. Change the Publication Status dropdown to Draft (Saved Internally). It will remain saved in the CMS without showing up on the public catalogue.

Will website visitors see technical jargon?

No. When hovering over technical terms (e.g. Search Engine Summary), the CMS shows a friendly 45ms explanation tooltip explaining how it impacts Google rankings.

How do customers search by standard?

Visitors can go to /products/ and type any IS standard (e.g. IS 13311 or IS 516) to see all conforming equipment immediately.

Can I add offline or phone enquiries?

Yes. In /admin/enquiries/, click + Log New Enquiry to log customer phone calls or walk-in orders into your centralized CRM.
