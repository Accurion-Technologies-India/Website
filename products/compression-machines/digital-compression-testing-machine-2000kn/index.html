Technical Overview & Compliance

The Accurion ACC-CTM-2000D Digital Compression Testing Machine is a four-pillar high-stiffness frame engineered for compressive strength determination of standard 150 mm concrete cubes and 150 x 300 mm cylinders in accordance with IS 516 and BS EN 12390-4.
