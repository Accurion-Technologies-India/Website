Technical Overview & Compliance

The Langry GZ Series Calibration Test Anvil is an indispensable reference instrument for any lab operating concrete test hammers. Machined from premium high-carbon tool steel and hardened to HRC 60 ± 2, it guarantees accurate daily calibration checks before and after structural inspections.
