Comprehensive Concrete Diagnostic Hierarchy

Complete non-destructive investigation of existing structural concrete relies on the combined correlation of three complementary disciplines: surface hardness (Rebound Hammer), internal volumetric pulse velocity (UPV), and subsurface reinforcement geometry (Rebar Locators).

🔨 Rebound Hammer Method

Governed by IS 13311 Part 2 and ASTM C805. Non-destructive surface hardness index for rapid compressive strength estimation.

📡 Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV)

Governed by IS 13311 Part 1 and ASTM C597. Volumetric core velocity indicating internal porosity, macro-cracks, and honeycombs.

🔍 Rebar Scanning & Covermeters

Governed by BS 1881-204 and ACI 318. Maps reinforcement distribution, verifies protective concrete cover, and prevents steel collision during core cutting.
