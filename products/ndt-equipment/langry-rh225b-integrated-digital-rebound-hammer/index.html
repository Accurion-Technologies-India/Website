Technical Overview & Compliance

The Langry RH225-B Integrated Digital Rebound Hammer combines mechanical rebound precision with digital data capture. Designed for rapid evaluation of compressive strength in concrete structures, it eliminates human reading errors through a contactless metal grating sensor. Measurements are displayed instantly on a 2.8-inch high-contrast OLED screen, broadcast via human voice prompts, and logged into onboard memory with Bluetooth field printing capability.
