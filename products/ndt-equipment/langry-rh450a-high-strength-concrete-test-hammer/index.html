Technical Overview & Compliance

The Langry RH450-A High-Strength Concrete Test Hammer is designed to overcome saturation issues encountered when standard 2.207 J hammers test concrete above 60 MPa. With a rated impact energy of 4.5 Joules, it accurately measures compressive strengths up to 100 MPa in high-rise building pillars, pre-stressed highway girders, and heavy infrastructure projects.
