About Us

Who We Are

Delhi's Trusted Civil Lab Equipment Supplier

Accurion Technologies is a trusted manufacturer, supplier, and service provider of complete Civil Quality Laboratory Equipment and NABL-accredited calibration solutions. Based in Delhi, we proudly serve customers nationwide with precision testing instruments and technical support.

We specialize in turnkey testing laboratory setups for Concrete, Cement, Soil, Bitumen, Aggregates, Steel, Water, and Construction Materials. From equipment supply to installation guidance and after-sales service, we provide end-to-end laboratory solutions tailored to your standards.
