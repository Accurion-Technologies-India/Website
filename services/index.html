Our Services

Beyond equipment supply, Accurion Technologies provides end-to-end support services to keep your laboratory running at peak accuracy and compliance. From NABL calibration to complete lab commissioning and ongoing maintenance.

SERVICE 01

NABL Accredited Calibration Services

Our NABL-accredited calibration services ensure that your testing instruments deliver accurate, traceable, and reliable results — meeting national and international quality standards.

We calibrate a wide range of civil laboratory equipment on-site or at our facility, providing comprehensive calibration certificates with full uncertainty analysis.

  • Calibration for CTM, UTM, and compression testing machines
  • Weighing balances and precision instruments
  • Temperature measuring instruments
  • Dimensional measuring equipment
  • NABL certificate provided with each calibration
  • On-site calibration available across India
