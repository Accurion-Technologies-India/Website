Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: 2025

1. Introduction

Accurion Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"), based in Delhi, India, is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our website visitors, clients, and business partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, use, and safeguard personal information collected through our website (www.accuriontechnologies.com), in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and internationally recognized data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when making product enquiries, requesting quotes, or contacting our technical team. This includes:

  • Full Name
  • Company / Organization Name
  • Phone Number and/or WhatsApp Contact
  • Email Address
  • Details of your equipment requirements, testing scope, or project specifications

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is utilized exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Responding to your product, pricing, or calibration enquiries
  • Preparing and sending formal quotations and equipment technical data sheets
  • Coordinating delivery, installation, commissioning, or maintenance services
  • Maintaining customer communication regarding order fulfillment and calibration renewals

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Our website utilizes Formspree (a secure form delivery service) to route contact messages to our official sales team email. We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing companies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your submitted personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Customer information is stored only for as long as necessary to service your enquiries and commercial orders.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We use browser localStorage solely to remember your preferred visual display mode (Light Mode / Dark Mode).

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections or updates, or request the deletion of your contact records from our active enquiry databases.
